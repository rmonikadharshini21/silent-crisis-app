Campus safety prototype

Help that doesn't wait for a button press.

If a student falls while alone and can't reach a phone, Silent Crisis notices through their own webcam and alerts someone they trust, with a location.

Create an account Log in

How it works

  1. You choose whenAccept the consent notice and start monitoring. A clear indicator shows it's on.
  2. A fall is noticedBody pose rules look for a sudden drop, then a low position with little movement.
  3. 10 seconds to cancelIf you're fine, enter your PIN and nobody is told.
  4. The right people knowYour contact gets the alert and location. Campus staff track it through to resolution.

Your privacy is built in

  • Monitoring runs only after you turn it on.
  • Video stays on the device. It is never saved or uploaded.
  • No face recognition and no shared cameras.
  • Your name, time and location are shared only when an alert goes out.

What it can't do

  • It only sees what's in the webcam's view, so it can't help on streets or buses.
  • It detects falls only, not harassment or other threats.
  • It gives a warning signal, not proof. Alerts say "possible fall, not confirmed".
  • It does not call police or an ambulance. People decide the response.

A hackathon prototype. Accuracy hasn't been tested in real conditions. In an emergency, call 112.
